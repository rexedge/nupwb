Default — name at 17/18px medium Ink, price 600 weight, tabular lining figures so the column aligns.
With description — Muted, one line, only where it earns its place. Most beers get none.
Finished, price known — row at 50%, Ribbon Red pill, price struck through. Never hidden: staff still get asked for it.
Finished, price unconfirmed — same dimming and pill, but an em-dash and no strike. Striking through a price that was never set says nothing.
Variants — parent carries the name and no price; labelled sub-rows indent 22px with a lighter rule.
Rows are 56px minimum, divided by a gold hairline at 20%. The dotted leader is the printed-menu convention — it is what lets a thumb track a name to its price in a dim room.

Search filters live across all seven categories and empties the sections that no longer match; category headings only survive if they still hold a row. Chips jump-scroll and take terracotta fill when active. Both stick under the nav — the search bar never leaves the screen on a page this long.
Palm Wine panel
The namesake category sits above the standard list in a double-ruled frame — 1px Gold outer, 1px Light Gold inner, uli corners on all four corners at desktop — echoing the logo's frame without redrawing it. It is the only panel on the site allowed that treatment; every other category uses the plain Card White panel so the hierarchy stays legible.
Prices render from the seed file's kobo integers. Chapman (Non-Alcoholic) and Shisha carry todoConfirmPrice — they show as Finished rather than displaying a price nobody has confirmed.

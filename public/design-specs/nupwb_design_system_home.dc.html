Decisions carried into screens 2 – 8
The nav is Card White, not Palm Green — the supplied logo is a full-colour emblem on white, and a cream bar lets it sit natively instead of in a knocked-out box. Green still owns the bands, buttons and footer. The akwete strip along the nav's bottom edge is what keeps it reading as brand chrome.
Prices come from the seed file in kobo and are formatted with tabular lining figures so ₦ columns align down a 150-row menu. Items with todoConfirmPrice show the Finished flag rather than a fabricated figure.
Address, phone and hours are realistic Enugu placeholders standing in for the TODO_CONFIRM venue settings. Igbo appears in the hero tagline only.

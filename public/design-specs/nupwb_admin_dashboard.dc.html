Admin posture
No Ankara anywhere except 5% behind the login card, and one 8px Akwete band under the admin header. Everything else is plain Card White on Cream. The palette and type carry the brand; the ornament would only slow down someone editing a price at 11pm.
Tap targets: 48px minimum, 56px for primary and destructive. Drag grips are a full 48px square. Bottom tabs sit in the thumb arc rather than behind a hamburger.
Every field label sits above its input, and the login error appears above the fields rather than beneath the button, so it is visible without scrolling on a 375px screen.
One deviation from the brief, on purpose: the active bottom tab is marked with a gold 3px indicator and a gold-tinted icon, but its label stays Palm Green. Gold text on Card White is 2.4:1 and fails AA — the gold marks the state, the green carries the word.
Delete never happens in one tap. The sheet leads with Move items to another category, states the real item count from the category, and puts Cancel last where the thumb rests.
